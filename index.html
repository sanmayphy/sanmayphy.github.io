Dr. Sanmay Ganguly

Assistant Professor, Department of Physics

Indian Institute of Technology, Kanpur

I am an experimental high energy physicist working at the frontier of particle physics and machine learning. My research focuses on the CMS experiment at CERN's Large Hadron Collider, where I work on heavy-flavor tagging, Higgs physics within and beyond the Standard Model, Jet-MET, and machine learning for particle reconstruction and simulation. I currently serve as convener of the CMS JetMET algorithm group (2025–2027).

Research Interests

CMS Experiment & Jet-MET

Experimental research with the CMS detector at CERN's Large Hadron Collider. I convene the CMS JetMET algorithm group (2025–2027).

Machine Learning for Reconstruction

Graph neural networks, set transformers and hypergraph models for particle flow, vertexing and event reconstruction.

Heavy-Flavor Tagging

Deep-learning jet flavor tagging at the LHC, including the DL1r and GN2 taggers developed in ATLAS.

Higgs Physics

The Higgs coupling to charm quarks, di-Higgs and tri-Higgs production, Higgs self-interaction, and searches for additional Higgs bosons.

Jet Physics & QCD

Jet cross sections, the jet energy scale, the running of the strong coupling, jet substructure and the Lund jet plane.

Generative AI & Fast Simulation

Generative models for detector response, including the CMS High-Granularity Calorimeter, and configurable calorimeter simulation for AI.

Explainable & Interpretable AI

Understanding what jet taggers learn, using explainability methods, mechanistic interpretability and model stitching.

Quantum Computation

Quantum machine learning and quantum-annealing methods for particle tracking at the LHC.

Real-Time & Fast Computing

Machine-learning reconstruction on FPGAs for the CMS Phase-2 upgrade and fast jet finding in Julia.

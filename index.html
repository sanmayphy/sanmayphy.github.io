Research Interests

Experimental High Energy Physics

Working on the CMS experiment at CERN's Large Hadron Collider, focusing on precision measurements and searches for new physics phenomena beyond the Standard Model. Currently serving as the Jet-MET algorithm sub-group convener.

Machine Learning in HEP

Developing and applying cutting-edge machine learning techniques including Graph Neural Networks, particle flow algorithms, and generative models for particle physics applications.

Higgs Physics

Studying Higgs boson properties, charm-tagging techniques, charm Yukawa coupling measurements, and constraints on Higgs self-interactions at the LHC.

Quantum Computation

Exploring applications of quantum computing in high energy physics, including quantum algorithms for simulation and optimization problems in particle physics.

Jet Reconstruction

Developing advanced jet energy scale measurements, particle flow algorithms, and reconstruction techniques for collider experiments.

Calorimeter Simulation

Creating configurable calorimeter simulations for AI applications and developing generative models for detector response simulation.
